# Chaotic, editable colour and UHD output

Keep the sparse current chaos at 0; continuously restore the earlier nested-domain-warp texture at 1. A single shader interpolates octave detail, spatial scale and fold weight. Cinematic palette selection supplies a starting value; manual adjustment and saved presets retain the value.

Colour slots show the active palette. Entering custom editing captures the live palette and its nonuniform role spacing, stops automatic colour motion, and exposes each HEX independently. Named presets persist complete settings on the local server with an atomic write; JSON import/export remains available.

Lighting uses broad surface normals, directional shadows and a restrained neutral highlight on the occupied forms. It does not blur the full-resolution field. Fullscreen UHD re-renders both the procedural field and final output inside a 3840 × 2160 envelope at full precision; the stored windowed performance settings return on exit. UHD is enabled by default and can be disabled in settings.
